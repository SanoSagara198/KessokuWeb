# Kessoku Website — Production Image Prompt Library

## Purpose

These prompts define the first production-media pass for the Kessoku website. Every image must feel like original game key art rather than a generic technology illustration. The images should communicate player fantasy immediately while leaving clean areas for website typography.

Use the filenames and crops in `assets/media/manifest.json`.

## Shared art direction

Apply these rules to every prompt:

- Original Roblox game-world aesthetic with premium cinematic rendering; do not imitate or reproduce copyrighted characters, logos, interfaces, maps, or franchise-specific assets.
- Stylized physical materials, readable silhouettes, strong composition, and game-ready environmental scale.
- No words, typography, logos, watermarks, HUD elements, brand marks, or fake interface panels inside the generated image.
- No excessive bloom, generic cyberpunk clutter, chromatic aberration, or random neon signage.
- Moderate cinematic atmosphere, physically coherent lighting, visible environmental depth, and restrained color grading.
- Website-safe composition: retain important subjects inside the central 80 percent and preserve the requested negative-space area.
- Avoid graphic gore. Combat and danger may be intense, but the presentation should remain suitable for a broad Roblox audience.
- Output as one single frame, not a collage, contact sheet, split screen, or multi-panel layout.

## Consistency workflow

After approving each primary game hero:

- Reuse that image as the visual reference for the related secondary frames.
- Preserve character faces, armor construction, vehicle design, city materials, lighting ratios, and palette.
- Change the scene and camera while retaining the project identity.
- Generate at final aspect ratio rather than cropping a square source.
- Export WebP at approximately 82–88 quality and verify the result remains below the media budget in `manifest.json`.
